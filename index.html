Connect your streaming apps

THECREATOR LIVE renders a clean, local browser feed. To use it in another app, capture the pop-out window in OBS, then send OBS Virtual Camera to your destination.

  1. 1. Start the preview
    Allow camera access or use the built-in studio demo feed. Turn on Virtual Cam, then choose Pop-Out Clean Feed.
  2. 2. Add the clean feed to OBS
    Add a Window Capture source for the borderless THECREATOR LIVE feed. Crop/fit as needed, then click Start Virtual Camera in OBS.
  3. 3. Select the camera in your app
    Choose OBS Virtual Camera in TikTok LIVE Studio, Zoom, Discord, or WhatsApp Desktop. Availability depends on your OS and app.

This page does not install or register an OS camera device. Browser support and third-party app capture options vary; never broadcast without checking your selected source and consent.
